PromptClip

PromptClip is a clipboard manager for Mac, built for those who use AI tools, write extensively, or copy and paste throughout the day. It maintains your full clipboard history, saves reusable snippets, and enables instant pasting via keyboard shortcuts.

Full Clipboard History

Access up to 100 recent clipboard items. Nothing gets lost, ensuring you always have what you need at your fingertips.

Permanent Snippets

Save prompts, templates, and snippets for permanent storage. Reuse them instantly without searching or recreating.

⌘1–⌘8 Shortcuts

Paste any saved snippet with a single keystroke. ⌘1 through ⌘8 provide direct access to your most-used items.

Universal Compatibility

Local and Lightweight

How It Works

Step 1: Copy as Usual

Paste anything into PromptClip automatically. Your full history builds in the background, up to 100 items.

Step 2: Save Snippets

Pin frequently used items as permanent snippets. Ideal for AI prompts, templates, or repetitive text.

Step 3: Assign Shortcuts

Map your top 8 snippets to ⌘1–⌘8. Customize for instant access to exactly what you need.

Step 4: Paste Anywhere

Trigger a shortcut in any app. PromptClip pastes directly—no menus, no hassle.
